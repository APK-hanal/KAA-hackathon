How Scams Actually Happen

Common pattern of fraudulent recruitment operations.

1

Unsolicited Offer

A sub-agent or social media post promises high wages abroad with guaranteed selection.

2

Pressure & Urgency

You are pushed to pay cash deposits immediately under claims that visa slots expire today.

3

Payment Without Contract

Funds are transferred to personal accounts without legally binding DoFE documentation.

4

False Departure

Workers arrive on tourist visas or face non-existent jobs without legal protection.

Official Data Source & Status Meaning

Agency licensing records are queried directly from public data published by Nepal's Department of Foreign Employment (DoFE). Status designations reflect official government database parameters:

Active & Registered Agency holds a valid active license with verified phone and location records.
Incomplete Contact Data Listed as active in registry, but missing verified phone or office records.
Not Found / Suspended Entity is not in DoFE records or operating under a canceled license.
