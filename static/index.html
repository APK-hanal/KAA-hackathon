Warning Signals

Top 4 Foreign Employment Scam Patterns

Advance Cash Without Official Receipt

Demanding cash advances prior to issuing pre-approval labor permits or official receipts is prohibited under Nepal law.

Unregistered Individual Sub-Agents

Working through individual brokers who lack official agency authorization letters or office locations carries high financial risk.

Travel on Tourist / Visit Visas

Promising to convert a visit visa into a work visa after departure bypasses DoFE insurance and legal protections.

Exaggerated Salary Guarantees

Promising wages significantly higher than official bilateral agreements without requiring skill verification.

Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if an agency is not found in the database?

If a searched name scores below our 80% similarity threshold against official DoFE records, Agency Shield automatically flags it as NOT FOUND IN DOFE REGISTRY. This triggers an automatic High Risk precaution warning because operating foreign employment recruitment without a government license is illegal in Nepal.

What is the "Free Visa, Free Ticket" policy in Nepal?

Under Nepal government guidelines for major Gulf countries and Malaysia, foreign employers are required to cover visa and airfare costs. Manpower agencies can charge a maximum service fee capped by law (typically 10,000 NPR) only when an official receipt is provided.

How does the feature phone SMS verification work?

Users without smartphones or internet access can text an agency name or license number to our SMS gateway. The system runs an instant lookup and replies with license status, district, and contact details in plain text.

Are community safety reports verified by government officials?

Community reports submitted on Agency Shield represent crowdsourced feedback from jobseekers. While registry license data comes directly from DoFE, community reports provide supplementary crowdsourced warning signals.
